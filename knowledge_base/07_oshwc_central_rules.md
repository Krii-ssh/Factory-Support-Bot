# OSH&WC (Central) Rules, 2026 — what the employer must do after a death

> **Why this is its own file.** `ingest.py` reads `sphere` from document
> frontmatter only. Held inside a `sphere: national` document, this content
> ingested as national and **displaced the correct s.85 answer on the
> contractor question** — leading a Ludhiana family with Central Rules that do
> not bind their employer. One `sphere` per file; see `06_`'s note.

> ⚠️ **These are the CENTRAL Rules.** They bind where the **Central
> Government** is the appropriate government. A hosiery, dyeing or steel unit
> in Ludhiana is normally **state sphere**, and Punjab has not notified OSHWC
> rules as far as we have established. RULE 5 of the gate drops this file
> unless central sphere is confirmed for the specific establishment.
>
> It stays indexed because it is the correct answer where it does apply, and
> because it documents the shape of the obligation — but a Ludhiana family
> must not be told FORM-XI binds their factory.

## chunk: employer_duty_inform_family

*Families ask: why did nobody tell us? We found out from another worker. The
factory never informed us. Nobody from the company called.*

**Rule 7(1), OSH&WC (Central) Rules, 2026.** Where an accident in an
establishment results in **death**, the employer shall inform the
Inspector-cum-Facilitator **forthwith** in **FORM-XI**, and shall inform
electronically and by telephone:

- the Chief Inspector-cum-Facilitator and Inspector-cum-Facilitator(s),
- the **District Magistrate or Sub-divisional Officer**,
- the **officer-in-charge of the jurisdictional police station**, and
- **the family members or kin of the victim**.

**Rule 7(2)** — where the accident causes bodily injury preventing work for
forty-eight hours (48 hours) or more, notice in FORM-XI within twelve hours
(12 hours) after the completion of those forty-eight hours.

> The duty to inform the family is **express**, not implied. A family that
> learned of the death from a co-worker, or hours later, or not at all, has a
> concrete and documentable grievance — and the same rule required the
> employer to call the police station, which is checkable against whether an
> FIR exists.

## chunk: form_xi_notice_of_accident

*Families and volunteers ask: what form should the factory have filed? Is
there a record of the accident? What paperwork proves it happened?*

**FORM-XI** under the OSH&WC (Central) Rules, 2026 is the "**Notice of
accident or dangerous occurrence**". It is the document the employer was
required to send to the Inspector-cum-Facilitator on a death.

> Ask whether it was filed. If it was, it is evidence of the accident's
> circumstances recorded by the employer at the time. If it was not, that is
> itself a breach sitting alongside whatever caused the death.
>
> **Sphere caveat applies** — for a state-sphere Ludhiana factory the form
> number and authority will follow the Punjab rules once notified.
