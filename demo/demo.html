How to read this. The left column is what a grieving family would actually have received on WhatsApp. The right column is the audit trail a legal reviewer checks, which chunks were retrieved, which gates fired and whether the answer model was called at all. Prompt rules only is the first working run, where the safety rules were instructions to the model. With output validator is the same eight questions after a deterministic check was added between the model and the user. Case 7 is the one to look at last: it was answered correctly by accident, and still cost an API call it should never have made.
